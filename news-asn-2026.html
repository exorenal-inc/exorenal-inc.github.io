Exorenal will exhibit at ASN Kidney Week 2026, the American Society of Nephrology's annual meeting. The meeting runs October 22–25 at the Colorado Convention Center in Denver, with the exhibit hall open October 22–24.

At the booth you can see the 29 kg XKIDNEY hemodialysis system up close and talk with our team. To book a meeting ahead of time, get in touch below.
