How it works

Pump-controlled. More precise.

MeasureFresh and spent dialysate flows are measured continuously.
ControlAPB (Active Pump Balancing) adjusts pump speed directly to match the two.
RemoveOnly the prescribed fluid is removed, more precisely than legacy balancing-chamber designs.
Setup

Familiar lines. Simple setup.

Uses the dialyzers and blood lines clinics already stock. The screen walks through each step, so it is easy to learn and simple to use.
