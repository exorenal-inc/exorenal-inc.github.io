Structural barriers to home HD adoption.

Despite strong clinical evidence, patients, service providers and VBC providers each face a barrier.

Questions

Investor FAQ.

What stage is exorenal at?

Working prototypes and bench validation data are in hand, and FDA 510(k) is in preparation. The target is 2027.

What is different?

APB (Active Pump Balancing) controls the pumps directly to balance fluid. 29 kg, with ±30 mL/h UF accuracy against an industry range of ±40–100 mL/h, on standard disposables.

How does it make money?

Equipment, service and software together, starting with hospitals and expanding to home and ICU.
