A paradigm shift toward value-based care.

CMS is moving U.S. kidney care toward value-based care. VBC operators have raised about $2.5B to reduce ICU costs, manage crash dialysis patients and improve planned home starts.

Structural barriers to home HD adoption.

Despite strong clinical evidence, patients, service providers and VBC providers each face a barrier.

Patients & caregiversCognitive and emotional burdenQuasi-clinician roles for setup and troubleshooting, anxiety about alarms and hypotension, space, noise and supplies, and 4–8 weeks of training.→ Light to move, automated steps, precise fluid removal
Service providersSafety risks remainNo real-time visibility into home treatments, hard to detect deterioration, and growing burden without matching reimbursement.→ Software automation keeps treatment at home stable
