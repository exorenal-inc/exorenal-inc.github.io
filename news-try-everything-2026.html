Try Everything 2026, Seoul.

We showed XKIDNEY at Seoul's global startup festival.

Date
Sep 9–10, 2026
Venue
DDP, Seoul
Booth
A-14 · Bio Health

Exorenal joined Try Everything 2026, Seoul's global startup festival, held September 9–10 at Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP). At booth A-14 in the Bio Health zone, we showed XKIDNEY under the theme "Next-gen hemodialysis beyond time and place."

Investors, clinicians and students stopped by to see the system up close and talk with our team about taking dialysis beyond the clinic. Thank you to everyone who visited.
